# MAVI PoC — known limits

**Profile:** `poc-single-host` (ADR-020). **Not Production-qualified. Evidence class: Development.** Every capability below is a proof of concept; its results are candidate evidence, never operational truth.

This is the PoC's one record of what each capability does not do (register row D3). The first part is a five-minute read; the reference detail after it gives each capability's limits in full. Every number comes from the acceptance register (`docs/reviews/2026-10-28-poc-acceptance.md`), whose row is named beside it. The operator UI carries the same statements on the System / About page (`src/web/mavi-web/src/features/system/PlateKnownLimits.tsx` and `FaceKnownLimits.tsx`, which tests hold to the numbers here; a change to either must change both).

## Read this first

- **What the PoC is.** One Windows host, recorded video only, fully offline at run time. It demonstrates every roadmap capability built in the PoC (Stages 2–10 on recorded video; live ingestion and alerting are not built) end to end, each within the measured limits below (Ask MAVI's translation bar is not met, C9.2), on a 13-clip demo corpus (Wikimedia Commons CC0/CC BY and MEVA CC BY 4.0). It is not Production qualification (Task 18), and no row of it claims model selection or identity.
- **The demo corpus has no ground truth** (register B5). A Track, plate read, attribute, event or candidate on a demo clip shows the pipeline working on pipeline output; it is not an accuracy figure. The only accuracy figures are the ones below, each on the data named.
- **Candidates, never identity.** Similarity, faces and cross-camera links are candidates with a score. Nothing links Tracks or names a person except an operator's audited confirmation, and even a confirmed Entity is "not identification".
- **Not in the PoC:** live RTSP/VMS ingestion and live alerts (next phase, plan D8); Indian plates; a learned attribute model; a specialist ReID model; Production hosts.

## Reference detail

### Face intelligence (Stage 5A; slice S6, register rows C5A.1–C5A.5)

- **Face candidates, never identification.**
  - Every face result is a candidate: a cosine score next to the reference threshold and the model identity.
  - "Find this face" lists candidate Person Tracks. A gallery comparison lists candidate gallery matches.
  - Nothing stores a match or writes an `EntityId`, and nothing says "this is <name>" (ADR-022).
- **Usable faces are rare in the demo corpus.** Protocols `s6-face-coverage-v1` (12 clips) and `s6-face-coverage-v2` (the A6 clip s2-13): YuNet score ≥ 0.9, face shorter side ≥ 32 px.
  - **7 of 140** Person Tracks (5 %) have a usable face, all in the two indoor MEVA clips: the stairwell s2-12 (2 of 9) and the entrance hall s2-13 (5 of 30).
  - The cafe lobby s2-10, the school hall s2-11 and every traffic clip have none.
  - Most Person Tracks are therefore **Unknown (no usable face)**, which means "not analysable", not "no match".
  - **Demonstration scope.** The demonstration opts in s2-10, s2-11, s2-12 and s2-13 (ADR-022 §3 as amended 2026-10-30; the owner confirmed G420 consent, D-S6-4). "Find this face" across s2-12 and s2-13 (same MEVA slot) works only for an actor whose face is usable in both, and no identity label confirms it is the same actor.
  - The detector threshold stays at 0.9: the predeclared v2 rule found only 77 % of the 0.8–0.9 band to be faces, under its 90 % bar (`docs/qualification/poc/s6-face-coverage.md`).
- **Opt-in per camera.** Face analysis runs only on cameras an operator has switched on. In the demonstration that is only the MEVA clips with consented actors.
- **Model and threshold.**
  - AdaFace IR-50 scores 99.8 % on LFW 10-fold and 99.7 % with faces downscaled to 32 px.
  - The reference threshold is cosine 0.2121, the 32-px operating point at 0.1 % false accepts on LFW (`docs/qualification/poc/s6-face-embedder-spike.md`).
  - LFW is frontal and well lit, and it may overlap the training identities. **The threshold is not calibrated on CCTV.**
  - On the demo clips, two usable faces of the same Track scored 0.14, below the threshold (`s6-face-e2e-rehearsal.md`). A candidate below the threshold is not evidence of a different person, and one above it is not evidence of the same person.
- **Research-only checkpoint.** The AdaFace weights follow WebFace4M's non-commercial research terms. The pack is Development-only and may be used only for non-commercial demonstration. SFace (Apache-2.0) is the built fallback.
- **Biometric data stays on this machine.** Face templates, the gallery and query images live only in this installation's database:
  - query images are erased once analysed;
  - query vectors are purged after 1 hour;
  - `Uninstall-MAVI-PoC` removes all of it.
  - Every face search, comparison and gallery change is attributed to the operator and audited.

### Find similar and appearance search (Stage 5; slice S4, register rows C5.1–C5.4)

- **Similarity candidates, never identity.** Results are labelled *Similarity candidates*; nothing writes an `EntityId` (ADR-021).
- **A general model, not ReID.** SigLIP 2 base is a general image–text model. On the frozen confirmatory sanity test (200 held H3 crops ≥ 80 px) it reached R@1 0.81 against a 16×16 thumbnail baseline's 0.785 (MRR 0.849 vs 0.835): a narrow margin, not a statistically meaningful difference. The first frozen test, on crops ≥ 24 px, failed its baseline clause.
- **Small crops are excluded.** A Track with no crop of at least 80 px gets "similarity unavailable" (55 % of the Tracks in the S4 run).
- **Text→image appearance search is unvalidated** beyond determinism and class ordering.
- **Across cameras the top results are look-alikes.** In the S4 run 11 of 18 similarity queries had a candidate from another camera in their top 10; these are look-alikes of the same class, not verified identities.
- **Exact scan.** Search is exact, with no approximate index (ADR-021 §4); latency was measured at demo scale only (similar p50/p95 12.6/38.4 ms, appearance 13.7/21.6 ms).

### Entity candidates and confirmed Entities (Stage 6; slice S7, register rows C6.1–C6.2)

- **Cross-camera links are operator-confirmed candidates, never automatic and never identification.**
  - MAVI proposes *Entity candidates*: pairs of Person or Vehicle Tracks on **different cameras that declare the same site**, whose appearance vectors score at or above the floor (0.85) and that are plausible in time. Each factor is shown separately (catalogue `mavi-entity-candidates-v2`, owner decision D-S7-3).
  - **Sites are declared, not inferred.** In the demonstration the six MEVA clips (one release, one capture slot) are one site and each Commons clip is its own. A camera with no declared site gets no candidates. Same-camera revisits and tracker-fragment links are not proposed, and the demo corpus has no vehicle candidates (its only MEVA vehicles are on the car park s2-09, which has no same-site vehicle partner).
  - Nothing is linked until an operator confirms a pair. Confirmation is the only thing that writes `Track.EntityId`; it is attributed to the operator and audited, and the Entity is labelled "Operator-confirmed Entity — not identification". Its identity status stays Unknown (ADR-023).
  - A candidate's appearance score comes from SigLIP 2, a general image–text model, not a re-identification model (ADR-021). On the frozen S4 record about one in ten different vehicles from different videos also reach the floor, so the list contains look-alikes of the same type and colour, which the operator must dismiss.
- **What the plausibility model does not know.** It reads only each Track's camera and UTC start and end. It excludes two Tracks on one camera at the same time, and two Tracks on different cameras that overlap by more than 2 s. It knows nothing else:
  - no camera locations, distances, routes or travel times: within a declared site, two cameras whose clips do not overlap in time are "plausible" whatever the distance between them;
  - no view overlap: two cameras that see the same spot will lose true pairs seen at the same moment for more than 2 s;
  - no clock error: it trusts each video's recording start time. (The seven Commons demo clips have adjacent synthetic start times from different cities; the site rule, not the time model, keeps them apart.)
  - only the six MEVA clips (s2-08 to s2-13) share one real time slot (2018-03-11 16:20), so only their cross-camera pairs can be real.
- **The face factor is separate and rare.** For a Person pair where both Tracks have a usable face, the operator can ask for the face score with its reference threshold (audited, not stored). It never ranks or gates candidates. With 7 of 140 demo Person Tracks having a usable face, most Person candidates show the face as Unknown. A face-only link (same face, different clothes) is not proposed. The face found in an evidence crop may be a bystander's: on the demo corpus the one face score above the threshold was on a pair that is clearly a man and a woman, so the face factor is something to inspect, not a verdict.
- **Workload and yield.** On the demo corpus v2 proposes 502 Person candidates across the MEVA cameras (the counts per camera pair are in the register, C6.1). A one-off diagnostic look at one crop per Track found 2 of the 10 highest-scoring candidates plausibly the same person, 4 that cannot be told (doorway silhouettes) and 4 clearly different: the appearance score ranks people in similar dark clothing together. The list is filtered by camera pair and ordered by appearance score. A per-Track bound (top 3, deterministic) would cut it to 127; it is a recorded option, not enabled.
- **After a reprocess, an Entity cannot grow on that video.** The new member must be plausible against every existing member, including the reprocessed video's earlier-run member, which is the same object at the same time; such a confirmation is refused, not forced.
- **Older installations.** A demo camera seeded before declared sites existed has none and cannot be updated, so it gets no candidates until the PoC is uninstalled (`Uninstall-MAVI-PoC`, which drops the database) and installed again; the seed and the demo import warn about it.
- **Entities are shown to operators only**, on every surface including Advanced Search (structured query) and Ask MAVI.
- **Corrections are recorded, never edits (S11-full, ADR-027 §4).** An operator can unlink a Track, reopen a dismissed candidate (or a confirmed one whose Tracks are no longer together) and merge two Entities; each is an audited decision appended to the Entity's history, and the earlier decisions stay visible. An Entity left with one or no member is kept. A merge, like a confirmation, is refused when any member of one Entity would be in two places at once with any member of the other; a merge is undone only by unlinking Tracks one by one.
- **Reprocessing carries nothing over.** A new run of a video has new Tracks with no Entity; earlier confirmations stay with the earlier run's Tracks and are readable through the Entity.
- **Repeated visit stays disabled.** Confirmed Entities now exist, but the entity-conditioned events rule waits for catalogue `mavi-events-v2` (ADR-023 §9).

### Plate reading (Stage 4; slice S3, register rows C4.1–C4.3)

- **Composition.** PD5 (PP-OCRv5 mobile detection, PaddlePaddle official ONNX, Apache-2.0) detects plates in a Vehicle Track's evidence crops; OC1-S (fast-plate-ocr `cct_s_v2_global`, MIT) reads them (plan decision D4). Both are unverified Development Model Packs; each licence is read from the pack's own LICENSE at install. They run on CPU (`plates-onnx-v1`); GPU plates are not available in the PoC yet.
- **Measured capability, proxy data only.** The Development pilot on the BDD100K H3 holdout (`docs/qualification/stage4/anpr-dev-pilot-2026-10-17.md`, Issue #225, PR #226): exact plate search returned the associated Track for **52/110** ground-truth vehicles with a usable plate text, and for **45/79** of those whose plate the reference reader could read (oracle-readable). The main losses are Track association and evidence that does not show the plate, not OCR. BDD100K is US dash-camera footage, not fixed CCTV.
- **No Indian plates.** No Indian plate data exists in MAVI's evidence, and no accuracy is claimed for Indian plates or for any data beyond the numbers above. An Indian plate corpus is post-PoC work (plan §7, O1B).
- **Demo-corpus behaviour is not a measurement.** The PoC demo clips carry no plate ground truth (register B5); a plate read on a demo clip shows the pipeline working, not its accuracy.
- **Candidates, not identifications.** A reading is a model output with a confidence. Check it against the plate crop and the source frame before acting on it. The Stage-4 acceptance register (`docs/reviews/2026-10-12-stage4-anpr-ocr-acceptance.md`, frozen) is unchanged: evidence adequacy INADEQUATE (53/79), no model selection, no Production claim.
- **Re-analysis is authorised by Windows identity on a trusted host.** `POST /api/plates/reanalysis` requires an ADR-010 operator and records the request against the operator's Windows account. On a PoC install every local user is an operator (`BUILTIN\Users`, owner decision S1-T1), so attribution names the account but does not restrict who may ask; a browser opened at `127.0.0.1` instead of `localhost` sends no Windows credentials and is refused (401).

### Visual attributes (Stage 2; slice S5, register rows C2.1–C2.2)

- **Zero-shot, Development.** The attributes are upper and lower clothing colour, carried bag, headwear and vehicle colour. They come from SigLIP 2 base (the S4 Model Pack, Apache-2.0, `unverified`), scoring each crop against text prompts with no training. This is not the Stage-2 learned attribute Model Pack (S2c), closes no Stage-2 F/G register row, and is not Production-qualified. The attribute rail, coverage line, every matched result row, and the inspector and Review panels say "zero-shot, uncalibrated, Development".
- **Candidates, not facts.** An attribute value is a model output with an abstention-normalised score (0.5 is the threshold, and the score is not a probability). Check it against the supporting crop before acting on it.
- **Unknown is not absent.** The model abstains (Unknown) when no value reaches its threshold. Bag and headwear are only ever `present` or Unknown: the PoC never claims a person carries no bag. A search never matches an Unknown or an unanalysed Track; the coverage line counts them instead.
- **Small crops.** A crop whose shorter side is under 80 px contributes zero scores (ADR-021 §1). These are averaged in and can only pull a Track towards Unknown, so distant subjects are usually Unknown.
- **Abstention is predeclared and uncalibrated** (owner decision S5-1, option D, 2026-10-30). Every attribute uses the same threshold, 0.5: a value is asserted only when it holds at least half of the model's score among the attribute's values. This threshold was fixed before any demo clip was processed and has not been calibrated on any data. A trial calibration on UPAR labels over PA-100K images shows why this matters (`docs/qualification/poc/s5-zero-shot-attributes.md`). Lower clothing colour reached only 0.38–0.45 precision at any threshold, and headwear could not be measured. Bag and upper colour reached about 0.8, but only on a different camera domain. That calibration is not shipped. Treat every value, and especially lower clothing colour, as a weak hint.
- **Search scale.** Attribute search is exact and measured only at demo scale; the Stage-2 E8 volume measurement is not attempted.

### Events (Stage 7; slice S8, register rows C7.1–C7.2)

- **What an event is.** A deterministic derivation from persisted scene-analytics facts by one rule of the versioned catalogue `mavi-events-v1` (ADR-025). No model decides an event. Each event names its rule version, scene revision, analytics algorithm version, the facts it came from and every parameter with its source. Events are only as good as the detections, Tracks and geometry beneath them. A fragmented Track (one vehicle split into several Tracks) produces several events.
- **Thresholds are disclosed, not tuned.** Dwell uses each zone's own threshold from the scene configuration. Stopped vehicle uses 30 s, the value the owner accepted for the demo after it was chosen post hoc (S2 admissibility record, decision 10). Here it is measured as the overlap of a stationary interval with a `Restricted` zone visit. Each qualifying interval is one event, so a vehicle that stands, moves briefly and stands again gives two events (on s2-09 one parked vehicle's two intervals, 3.4 s apart, give two of its six events). The overlap is measured against one zone visit at a time: if tracking jitter at the zone edge splits a stationary vehicle's visit into two shorter visits, each under 30 s, nothing fires. Merging such visits would be a new catalogue version. Co-occurrence (a Person and a Vehicle in one zone at the same time) has no threshold. No threshold was changed after reading the events.
- **Wrong-way is defined but configured on no demo clip.** A trip line needs a permitted direction for it to fire. No S2 scene sets one, so the rule is shown by its test fixture only.
- **Repeated visit is disabled.** It is entity-conditioned. Confirmed Entities exist since slice S7, but enabling the rule is a new catalogue version (`mavi-events-v2`, ADR-023 §9), not part of S7. It is defined and tested against a fixture, the UI shows it as disabled with the reason, and no entity is faked.
- **One run at a time.** v1 rules see one run against one scene revision. Nothing spans runs or cameras.
- **Demo-corpus behaviour is not a measurement.** The demo clips carry no event ground truth (register B5). An event fired on a demo clip shows the rule working on pipeline output, not its accuracy.

### Structured query and Advanced Search (Stage 8; slice S9, register rows C8.1–C8.2)

- **A closed language, on purpose.** Contract `mavi-query-v1` (ADR-024) has three units (Track, Event, Entity), a fixed list of predicates per unit, combined only with AND, and nine operations (`find`, `count`, `countDistinct`, `groupBy`, `timeBucket`, `first`, `last`, `top`, `compare`). There is no OR, no nesting, no join across units (for example "Tracks that have an event"), no arbitrary field and no user-defined metric. A question outside the contract is refused with a typed code, never approximated. Relative time ("today", "last hour") is not in the contract: the builder, or Ask MAVI (S10), resolves it to explicit UTC instants first.
- **The Entity unit reads confirmed Entities only.** Since S7 (C6.2) it reads operator-confirmed Entities, never candidates, and only for an operator; anonymous requests are refused.
- **Coverage gates every figure.** An answer about the whole scope (a count, groups, buckets, an extreme, a ranking, a comparison) is withheld when any part of the scope has not been analysed: scene analytics pending, failed or stale; events not yet derived; zero-shot attributes or plate reading not configured or not yet run. A list (`find`) still shows the real rows it found, marked as partial, and its total is then only a lower bound ("at least N so far"), never the scope's count. A camera with no scene configuration counts as incomplete for an Event query, so an Event question over every camera is withheld until every camera with recordings has a scene; a question naming a zone or line is covered only by the camera that owns it, and other cameras named in it are left out of its identity and coverage without a separate notice.
- **Completeness means analysed, not processed.** A Track query with no geometric or derived predicate reports complete coverage over the videos whose processing has finished and been published, as Track search does: a video still queued, processing or failed contributes no Tracks and is not reported as a gap. Check Processing before trusting a plain Track count over fresh imports.
- **Attributes stay zero-shot.** An attribute predicate matches only Observed values (zero-shot, uncalibrated, Development). Tracks whose attribute is Unknown are reported as abstentions, and Tracks whose crops could not be read for attributes (Unavailable) are reported beside them; neither is counted as a match or as a non-match. Attributes and a plate text cannot be combined in one query, as on Track search.
- **Time semantics.** A Track belongs to a window when it overlaps it, as in Track search, and is bucketed and ordered by its first instant inside the window. A Track with a line predicate is counted when it crossed the line at any time, not only inside the window; to count crossings inside the window, query the Event unit (`line-crossing`), which uses the crossing instant.
- **Groups are only the non-empty ones.** `groupBy` and `top` list a group only where at least one record exists. Under complete coverage a missing group is a zero (the events timeline lists a rule that ran with its zero; the query does not).
- **Bounded, not paged.** A query whose matching scope exceeds 50,000 records is refused (`query_scope_too_large`) rather than answered from a truncated set. `find` returns at most 200 rows with the full total. There is no continuation cursor in v1. A Track list that Search can express (at most one camera, the window and the class) links to Search, which pages it; other lists, groups and time buckets have no drill-through to their members yet. Event rows open Review at the event and the camera's Events page, which opens on its default window rather than the query's.
- **Not in v1.** The appearance text query (it waits on a worker vector, so it is not a deterministic read). Motion and similarity predicates exist in the contract and the API, but the Advanced Search builder does not offer them yet. Similarity (`similarTo`) is `find`-only and ranked by the S4 service in its own snapshot.
- **Latency is a Development observation.** The latency recorded on the demo corpus (register C8.2) was measured on the Development laptop over loopback, one query at a time, against a predeclared budget. It is not a performance qualification and says nothing about a Production host or a larger archive.

### Ask MAVI (Stage 9; slice S10, register rows C9.1–C9.3)

- **A closed question range, on purpose.** Ask MAVI answers only what one `mavi-query-v1` query can express (ADR-024, ADR-026): counts, lists, groups, time buckets, rankings, firsts and lasts and comparisons of Tracks and Events (and of confirmed Entities, for an operator), by time, camera, object class, the `car` subclass, zone, line and direction, zero-shot attributes, plate text, duration and event rule. It does not answer "why", identity ("who is this"), predictions, opinions, averages, speeds, OR between places, follow-ups that depend on an earlier answer, or anything that changes data. Those are refused, never approximated.
- **Refusal behaviour.** Every refusal is shown plainly with its stage and a closed code: the platform refused the question before the model because it names a place the site does not have (stage `question`, `unknown_reference`; prompt v2); the model refused (`outside_contract`, or `unknown_reference` for a camera, zone or line the site does not have); its output was not a translation document (`model_output_invalid`, `model_output_truncated`, `time_offset_mismatch`, or `plate_text_ungrounded` for a plate text the question does not contain); the strict S9 parser refused the query; or the language role is not available (`language_unavailable`, `language_timeout`). There is no retry, no repair and no fallback: a refusal is the answer.
- **Ask MAVI proposes, you confirm (D-S10-10).** A question returns a proposed query ("Interpreted as"), not an answer. Nothing runs until you press **Run**, which sends exactly that query to Advanced Search's service, or **Open in Advanced Search**, which loads it into the builder to change it first. Open is disabled, with the reason, for a query the builder cannot hold (a minimum duration, a minimum dwell or loitering, an unusual bucket size); Run still works. The page states the measured accuracy: on 40 independently written questions, 20 were interpreted exactly by the larger model and 19 by the laptop model. Read the proposal before you run it.
- **The model writes queries, never facts.** Nothing on the Ask page is model text. "Interpreted as" is the server's canonical query, with names read from the site catalogue; the answer is the S9 envelope, rendered with the same withheld, empty and partial states as Advanced Search; the one-line summary selects envelope values and never computes one. A wrong interpretation is possible and is visible: it can only be a valid query an operator could have built in Advanced Search.
- **What the model is allowed to write is narrowed by the question.** The decoding grammar offers only the cameras, zones, lines, line sides, attribute values, plate, rules and units the question names. Each unit (Track, Event, Entity) is offered only its own predicates, dimensions and fields. With prompt v2 (D-S10-9) a name matches by its words, with tighter rules for short names:
  - a one-word name ("Crossing", "Landing") is offered only when the question says "<name> zone" or "<name> line", so "on the Landing" does not reference the Landing zone;
  - a name that ends in its kind ("Count line") must be said as that phrase;
  - a line side labelled "In" or "Out" needs a verb ("went in") or a synonym;
  - "To door" and "From door" need their phrase;
  - a camera can also be named by part of its name before "camera" ("the Highway camera"), by a capitalised part of its name ("Macedonia Square"), or by its number ("camera 7").

  Gating only ever offers fewer choices than no gating would. An indirect reference that matches no name is not resolved, so say the configured name. The missing predicate is then visible in "Interpreted as", or the request is refused (D-S10-6, D-S10-9; ADR-026 §4 amendments).
- **A place the site does not have is refused, if it is said in one of three forms (prompt v2).** The platform refuses the question before the model, with `unknown_reference`, when it names something the site lacks in one of these forms:
  - words before "camera", "zone" or "line" ("the Loading dock zone");
  - such a word followed by a number or a capitalised name ("camera 15");
  - two or more capitalised words ("Central Station"), except in a question typed entirely in title case.

  A place said any other way ("near the bike shed", "the parking garage") is left to the model, which may answer for the whole site; "Interpreted as" then shows every camera. On the fresh held split the pre-check refused 2 of the 3 unknown places, with no false refusal on those 40 questions. It works on wording, not meaning, so a valid question can still be refused with `unknown_reference` when a word in it looks like a place name. Rephrase with the configured name.
- **Time.** Questions are read in the site time zone (the one zone every camera shares, or `Ask:SiteTimeZoneId`). "Today" means the reference date the operator sets (empty means the current site date); the demo corpus is recorded on 2026-10-01, so set that date for the demo. With prompt v2 the decoding grammar admits every offset the site zone uses in the year before the reference date, and the translator checks each instant's offset against the zone at that instant. So in a zone with daylight saving, windows across a change are expressed correctly (m8). The demo site, Asia/Kolkata, has no daylight saving.
- **Model sizes and their hardware.** Two Language Model Packs ship (D-S10-2): Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct Q8_0 (1.89 GB) for the Development laptop's GTX 1650 Ti (4 GiB, `sm_75`, through the driver's PTX JIT) or CPU, and Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct Q4_K_M (4.68 GB) for the RTX 4090 (`sm_89`, native code). The installer binds the 7B pack only on a GPU with 16 GiB or more. The laptop demonstrates the 1.5B pack only (D-S10-3). The 7B pack runs on the laptop only with most layers on the CPU, at minutes per question. **Prompt-prefix reuse is on for the 1.5B pack and off for the 7B pack** (D-S10-7). The 7B's prompt-v2 recording was not byte-identical when asked in reverse order. The installer writes `Language:CachePrompt` per pack (the API treats a missing setting as off), so the 7B reprocesses its whole prompt on every question: about 2 minutes per question on the laptop (median 117 s with 16 of 28 layers on the GPU). On a GPU that holds the model fully it is much faster, but this is not measured here. The 1.5B's reuse was checked on this laptop GPU only; on CPU or on the 4090 (S12) it is not yet checked, and the 4090 repeats the check.
- **The translation corpus is the only measured claim, and accuracy is about one question in two.**
  - **Prompt v2, fresh held split** (40 questions written blind; `contracts/test-vectors/ask-mavi-questions-v2.json`; D-S10-9; measured once on the Development laptop):
    - 7B pack **20/40 (50%)**, in contract 17/31;
    - 1.5B pack **19/40 (47.5%)**, in contract 16/31.
  - The 85% bar is not met, so C9.2 is not passed.
  - **Prompt v1, original held split:** 31/40 and 19/40. That split was written alongside the development questions and overstated accuracy.
  - **Typical errors**, all visible in "Interpreted as" and all valid queries an operator could have built:
    - a dropped predicate: "people" with an attribute, "cars", "crossing events";
    - a wrong unit or zone relation;
    - a minutes conversion;
    - answering a question the contract cannot express (OR between zones, a longest stay, a rename) instead of refusing it.
  - **Read "Interpreted as" before trusting an answer.**
  - Figures and evidence: the register (C9.2). They are exact-match rates of the canonical query, not an estimate of accuracy on other sites, phrasings or languages. Recordings are device-specific: the same pack can decode differently on CPU, on `sm_75` and on `sm_89`, and with prompt reuse on or off.
- **Plate text must come from the question.** With prompt v2 a plate text the model writes is accepted only when its letters and digits occur, in order and adjacent, in the question's letters and digits joined together; otherwise the request is refused (`plate_text_ungrounded`). This is a substring check, so a text built from adjacent words, or from a word such as "TODAY", passes it. It is still shown as "Plate text" in "Interpreted as".
- **Site size.** The prompt lists every camera, zone and line. A site whose catalogue does not fit the role's context (about two dozen cameras at the demo's density) is refused (`prompt_too_long`), never truncated.
- **CI does not run the model.** CI replays recorded outputs through the real prompt builder, translator and parser, and fails when the prompt, grammar or corpus changes without re-recording (`AskCorpusRecorder`). It proves the pipeline and the recorded run, not a fresh inference.
- **A stated exception to "Python owns AI inference".** The language role is a native llama.cpp process (`llama-server`) on 127.0.0.1, outside the .NET process, holding no authority (D-S10-5, ADR-026 §3).
- **Licences.** llama.cpp MIT; the CUDA runtime DLLs (`cudart`, `cuBLAS`) under the NVIDIA CUDA 12.4 EULA, Attachment A, retained with them; Qwen2.5 1.5B and 7B Apache-2.0. Licence review pending; the research-licensed 3B and 72B sizes are never used.

### Review decisions, cases and audit (Stage 10; slice S11-full, register row C10.2)

- **A review decision is about the detection, not the object.** "Confirm detection" says the Track and its class are correctly detected; "Reject" says they are not. Neither says who or which vehicle it is, and neither links Tracks (ADR-027 §1).
- **A case is a folder, not a finding.** Items in one case are not asserted to show the same person or vehicle. Adding Tracks to a case writes no Entity and no identity.
- **Nothing is edited.** Decisions, case notes and case items are append-only; a correction is a later recorded decision, and a removed case item stays visible with who removed it and why. The database refuses UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE on these tables; like the audit table (ADR-010 §6) this guards against application defects, not against the database owner.
- **The audit trail shows hashes, not payloads.** Each entry names the operator, how they were identified, the action, the subject and the server time; the submitted payload is kept only as its SHA-256 (ADR-010 §4). Under the `development-unattributed` fallback, actions are not attributable to a person and say so.
- **Reprocessing starts again.** A new run's Tracks are Unreviewed and belong to no Entity or case; the Review page says how many Tracks of an earlier run were reviewed and links to them. Nothing is copied.
- **Case references can go stale.** A case holds references, not copies; if a subject's evidence no longer exists it is shown as unavailable.

- **No review-status search filter yet.** Search shows each Track's review status but cannot filter on it. Search pages keep one snapshot across their cursor, while review status changes as operators decide, so a filter on it needs a change to the search cursor contract to page consistently; it is a follow-up, not part of S11-full.

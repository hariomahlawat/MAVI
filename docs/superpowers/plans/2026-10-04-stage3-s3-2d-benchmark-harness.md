**Governing:** ADR-017 (benchmark-first Development; §4 mappings, §6 association, §7 admissibility, §11 harness shape); ADR-016 (detector-native source, unchanged); ADR-015 (public-first Development, protected final qualification); the two current roadmaps; the S3.2 plan §14 amendment; the Stage-3 register (`docs/reviews/2026-10-03-stage3-vehicle-subclass-acceptance.md`, the only exit authority; this plan closes nothing). ## 1. Objective and non-goals

**Objective.** A reusable, deterministic benchmark harness that evaluates a MAVI capability against an existing labelled research dataset without new human labels: a shared envelope for identity and provenance, dataset adapters, capability-specific ground truth, prediction-independent association of ground-truth tracks to MAVI Tracks, a capability evaluator, and a reproducible result. Stage-3 vehicle subclass is the first consumer.

**Non-goals.** No change to the detector, Model Pack, pipeline profile (`1.3.0-candidate`, `afb03b6c…`), thresholds (`minShare` 0.6, `minMatchedDetections` 3), vocabulary or stored Track values. No API, search or UI exposure. No change to T1–T10 tooling semantics or T10 evidence. No dataset download in the planning task. No universal ground-truth schema. No qualification claim: every result is Development or reference evidence (ADR-017 §8–§9).

## 2. Repository facts the design rests on (read, not assumed)

- **T1 export** (`vehicle-subclass-measurement-export-v1`, `tools/dotnet/Mavi.MeasurementExport`): per run, `video` (`videoAssetId`, `cameraCode`, `sourceSha256`, `width`, `height`, `frameRateNumerator/Denominator`, `durationMs`), `profile.pipelineProfileSha256`, the run `attestation` (producer identity: `maviCommit`, `modelPackId`, `runtimePackId`, `pipelineProfileSha256`, `checkpointSha256`, `componentBindingSha256`, device, …), and `tracks[]` with `id`, `objectClass`, `startOffsetMs`, `endOffsetMs`, `detectionCount`, `objectSubclass` (nullable), `objectSubclassSource`, `objectSubclassVocabulary`, `trajectorySha256`, and `observations[]` (at most four: `sourceFrameNumber`, `videoOffsetMs`, normalised `boundingBox {x, y, width, height}`, evidence crop hash). The exporter copies crops, not trajectories.
- **Trajectory artefact** (`src/vision/mavi_vision/video/trajectory.py`, format v1): msgpack `{"v": 1, "points": [[offset_ms, center_x, center_y], …]}` with strictly increasing offsets and normalised centres in [0, 1]. **It carries no box size.** It is sealed at finalization under the evidence root as `evidence/{jobId}/attempt-NNNN/trajectories/{trackId}-{sha256}.msgpack` (`EvidenceSealingPlan.AcceptedEvidenceKey`), and the export's `trajectorySha256` names it. The export does not carry the vision job id, so the harness locates a trajectory by its hash (`*-{sha256}.msgpack` under the evidence root) and verifies the bytes.
- **Consequence for association.** Frame-level IoU between MAVI and ground truth is not available from stored data. Boxes exist only at the ≤ 4 observation frames. Association therefore uses per-frame **centre containment with nearest-centre exclusivity** across the whole overlap, **minimum temporal coverage on both sides**, and a **hard IoU gate at the observation frames** where real MAVI boxes exist (§7). MAVI-side purity is measured only on the **evaluable** MAVI points (those aligned to a labelled GT instant), so a 30 fps MAVI Track against 5 Hz labels is judged on its labelled instants, not penalised for its unlabelled frames (§7.3); the IoU gate uses the GT box at the observation time, exact when the frame is labelled and linearly interpolated between the two bracketing labelled frames otherwise (§7.2). Per-point boxes would make every frame IoU-checkable; that is a product change (trajectory v2) with a predeclared escalation trigger (§7.7), not a prerequisite for S3.2d-1.
- **Existing helpers** (`tools/stage3/artefacts.py`): canonical JSON, SHA-256, `write_once`, `OutputDirectory`, schema `validate`, `read_artefact`, `Export` (export reading and integrity checks), `load_exports` (duplicate run/video/source refusal), `git_binding`. `tools/phase1/evaluate_vehicle_subclass.py`: attestation validation (`_validate_attestation`), single-producer check (`_producer`), per-Track outcome (`_track_outcome`: subclass or `undetermined`), exact-fraction metrics. `tools/stage3/run_subclass_measurement.py`: `status()` with the support-floor-first ordering. `tools/stage3/ingest_source_pool.py`: a stdlib `Api` client for `GET/POST /api/cameras`, `POST /api/videos/import` (multipart), `POST /api/videos/{id}/process`, `GET /api/videos/{id}/processing`, and the export command invocation. `tools/stage3/media_tools.py` and `derive_mp4.py`: verified FFmpeg pack and MP4 derivation. `tools/qualification/attributes/datasets/mapping.py`: an existing label-mapping shape (`parse_mapping`, `mapping_sha256`, `apply_mapping`) for the S2c still-image datasets.
- **Dependencies.** `msgpack` is a vision-runtime dependency (declared in `config/dependencies/offline-dependency-policy-v1.json`) and is present in the repository venv; the tools surface (`tools/requirements.txt`) does not declare it. Reading trajectories from the harness adds it to the tools surface (Slice 2; AGENTS dependency rule). `scipy` is already a tools dependency; the association does not need it (§7.5).
- **Catalogue practice.** T9 ran on a fresh, dedicated Development catalogue and media store with process-scope configuration overrides (register F1). Benchmark runs follow the same practice: one dedicated catalogue and media store per dataset release, never the ordinary Development catalogue.
- **Store layout convention.** Stage-3 evidence lives under the controlled-store root `E:\MAVI-Controlled` with store-relative citations. Benchmarks use `E:\MAVI-Controlled\Benchmarks\<dataset>\<release>\` (§10). Dataset bytes never enter Git.

## 3. Architecture

```
dataset release ──adapter──▶ shared envelope (identity, release, split, provenance/admissibility, mappings,
                             exposure, MAVI execution identity, reproducibility)
                             + capability-specific canonical ground truth
                             (vehicle tracking/subclass: sequence, frame, time, box, native track id, native class)
        ──MAVI execution (import → process → T1 export; trajectories read by hash)──▶ MAVI Tracks
        ──capability-specific association (geometry and time only)──▶ matched / fragment / ambiguous / unmatched
        ──capability evaluator──▶ scope A: end-to-end coverage; scope B: Track-conditional subclass quality
        ──result + evidence (canonical, hashed, write-once)
```

**Shared core owns:** dataset and release identity; split identity; source and access identity; provenance and research-use status (ADR-017 §7, release-level); the native-taxonomy declaration and per-class mappings (§6); exposure metadata (§13); MAVI execution identity (producer from attestations, profile hash, export and trajectory hashes); run identity; the result envelope and its deterministic hash; reproducibility metadata (adapter, mapping, association-policy and runner identities). **Capability modules own:** the ground-truth record shape they need, the association geometry, the evaluator and the result body. Nothing capability-specific sits in the core; nothing dataset-specific sits in the evaluator.

## 5. Contracts

All artefacts follow the Stage-3 rules: canonical JSON, identity = SHA-256 of canonical bytes, write-once, no wall-clock field inside anything hashed (timestamps, where kept, live only in an unhashed sidecar or the run log).

**`benchmark-dataset-release-v1`** (release-level provenance and admissibility; ADR-017 §7): `schemaVersion`; `datasetId` (slug); `datasetName`; `release` (exact version or edition string); `task` (`box-tracking`, `detection`, …); `source` {`kind`: `official` | `author-repository` | `institutional-archive` | `research-mirror` | `challenge-archive`; `url`; `retrievedOn`; `credibilityBasis` (required when kind ≠ `official`)}; `access` {`mechanism`: `direct` | `registration` | `agreement` | `request`; `preconditions[]`}; `intendedUse` (`development-benchmarking`); `researchUse` {`status`: `RESEARCH-ADMISSIBLE` | `RESEARCH-UNCERTAIN` | `BLOCKED`; `basis`; `termsReference`; `redistribution`: `not-permitted-by-default`}; `splits[]` {`name`, `labelled`: bool, `role`}; `nativeTaxonomy[]` {`code`, `name`, `definition`}; `frameTime` {`kind`: `index-at-fps` | `per-frame-timestamp`; `fpsNumerator`, `fpsDenominator`}; `manifest` {`kind`: `file-hashes` | `archive-hashes`; `entries[]` {`path`, `sizeBytes`, `sha256`}} (frozen by `describe --freeze-manifest` after acquisition; **non-empty is required before `prepare`**, which verifies every consumed byte against it); `exposure` (§13). One record per release; a per-file review is not modelled, by design.

**`benchmark-class-mapping-v1`** (§6): `schemaVersion`; `datasetId`; `release`; `capability` (`mavi-vehicle-subclass-v1`); `mappings[]` {`nativeClass`, `maviClass` | null, `kind`: `exact` | `subset` | `unsupported`, `unsupportedKind` (required when `kind` is `unsupported`): `outside-capability` | `vehicle-unresolved`, `reason`, `evaluation`: `scored` | `subset-conditional` | `excluded`} (the evaluation value is implied by the kind in v1 and is written out so a reader sees it). Identity = hash of canonical bytes, recorded in every result.

**`benchmark-association-v1`** (§7 output): envelope reference; `policy` {`version`, `alignment`: `midpoint-windows-v1`, `minOverlapFrames`, `minContainment`, `minGtCoverage`, `minMaviCoverage`, `minSpotCheckIoU`, `ambiguityMargin`, `distanceAmbiguityMargin`, `minConsecutiveContainedFrames`} (no separate time tolerance: the window rule of §7.1 is the only alignment predicate); `sequences[]` {`sequenceId`, `videoAssetId`, `processingRunId`, `pairs[]` {`gtTrackId`, `maviTrackId`, `overlapFrames`, `containment`, `meanNormalisedDistance`, `gtCoverage`, `maviCoverage`, `spotCheckIoU[]`}, `ambiguousGt[]`, `fragmentedGt[]`, `mergedGt[]`, `unverifiedGt[]`, `unmatchedGt[]`, `ignoredGt[]`, `fragmentMavi[]`, `unverifiedMavi[]`, `unmatchedMavi[]`, `ignoredMavi[]`}, each non-pair entry carrying the evidence that produced it (candidate ids, containment, coverage, IoU); plus `alignment` {`sourceRate`, `labelledRate`, `evaluablePointsTotal`, `maviPointsTotal`}; and `associationBodySha256` = hash of the canonical bytes of `policy` + `sequences[]` + `alignment` alone (the class-free body). The envelope (run identity, ground-truth and mapping hashes) is provenance and legitimately changes when truth or mapping changes; the body hash is what the class-independence tests compare, and the whole-artefact hash remains the artefact's identity. **No class, subclass or confidence field anywhere in this artefact.**

**`benchmark-vehicle-subclass-result-v1`** (§8): the envelope; `associationSha256`; `mappingSha256`; `scopeA` (coverage) and `scopeB` (Track-conditional classification), each with its own `scope` statement; per-sequence breakdown; `requirements` reference where the support floor is taken from the registered requirements (`ca28702f…`); the same hash sits in the envelope's `tooling.requirementsSha256`, so a corrected or re-versioned requirements file cannot produce different `insufficient-support` outcomes under the same `benchmarkRunId`.

The envelope embedded in the association and result artefacts: `benchmarkRunId` (hash of the inputs below), `dataset` {`datasetId`, `release`, `split`, `descriptorSha256`}, `sequences[]` (ids, derived-video hashes and `groundTruthSha256` of each sequence's canonical prepared ground truth), `derivationManifestSha256`, `mavi` {`maviCommit`, `pipelineProfileSha256`, `producer` (the single attested producer), `exportSha256s[]`, `trajectorySha256s[]`}, `tooling` {`adapterId`, `adapterVersion`, `mappingSha256`, `associationPolicySha256`, `requirementsSha256` (the registered requirements the support floor is read from), `runnerVersion`, `toolingCommit`, `toolingSha256`}, `exposure` (§13). `toolingSha256` is computed at run time over the canonical bytes of every behaviour-bearing file the harness executes with, enumerated **by the import graph, not by a hand-maintained list**: after the CLI has imported its command module, every loaded module whose file lies under the repository's `tools/` tree (`sys.modules` filtered by path, which reaches `tools/benchmarks/**`, the `tools/stage3` helpers, `tools/phase1/evaluate_vehicle_subclass.py` for `_track_outcome`/`_producer`, `mavi_api.py`, `media_tools.py` and anything they import in turn) plus the four benchmark schemas and the association-policy and mapping schemas; the enumerated path list is written into the envelope beside the hash so a reviewer can see what was bound. `toolingCommit` is the HEAD of the running checkout, and the run is refused (`tooling_dirty`) when any enumerated file differs from that commit's blob (the `git_binding` check applied to the tooling itself). `adapterVersion` and `runnerVersion` stay as human-readable labels; the identity does not depend on anyone remembering to bump them, so a changed evaluator cannot reuse a `benchmarkRunId` or collide with a write-once result.

## 6. Mapping semantics

Per native class, never per dataset (ADR-017 §4; AGENTS item 3). Kinds:
- `exact`: the native definition coincides with one MAVI class under the labelling guide (`docs/qualification/stage3/s3-2-labeling-guide.md`, the reference for the four MAVI classes). `evaluation: scored`: counts in precision and recall of that class.
- `subset`: the native class lies entirely within one MAVI class but does not cover it (for example `sedan` → `car`). `evaluation: subset-conditional`: the evaluator reports, for that native class only, a subset-conditional block (the share of its assigned tracks on which MAVI resolved the mapped class, the `undetermined` share, and the confusion of MAVI outcomes on that subset). A subset track's truth **is judgeable**: a sedan is a car, so a MAVI `car` on it is a true positive for `car` precision and a MAVI `truck` on it is a false positive for `truck` precision; subset tracks therefore **enter class precision** under the explicit completeness rule below, and **never enter class recall** (a dataset that labels only sedans cannot say how many cars MAVI missed; the subset block carries the subset's own recall-like figure).
- `unsupported`: `evaluation: excluded` from classification scoring, but **always present** in coverage (scope A) and in the "excluded native classes" counts of scope B. Two sub-kinds, because they affect precision differently: `vehicle-unresolved` is a vehicle MAVI may well track and label, whose native class cannot be mapped without assumption (`van`: the guide splits vans by body; `others`; `tricycle`), so a MAVI class prediction on it can be neither confirmed nor refuted; `outside-capability` is a class that is not a Vehicle in MAVI's broad class (`pedestrian`, `rider`, `bicycle`, `train`), so a MAVI Vehicle Track on it is an association or detection error, visible in scope A, not an unjudgeable subclass prediction.

Rules the evaluator enforces: a MAVI class with no `exact` native class in the dataset gets `recall: not-in-dataset`, never a number, whatever `subset` classes exist for it (the exact-GT prerequisite applies to recall only); a MAVI class predicted on **no assigned track at all** gets `precision: no-judgeable-predictions`; the status is decided in this order: any C-prediction on a `vehicle-unresolved` track → `not-available` with the count (even when the judgeable denominator is empty), else no C-prediction anywhere → `no-judgeable-predictions`, else the computed ratio; **class precision is computed over every judgeable prediction and requires a complete negative universe for that class.** The GT truth of an assigned track is judgeable when its mapping is `exact` (truth = the mapped class), `subset` (truth = the mapped class) or `unsupported (outside-capability)` (truth = not a vehicle class, so any MAVI class prediction on it is a false positive); it is unjudgeable only for `unsupported (vehicle-unresolved)`. `precision(C)` = predictions of C on judgeable tracks whose truth is C ÷ all predictions of C on judgeable tracks, and it is **available only if no assigned `vehicle-unresolved` track received the prediction C** (every C-prediction is then judged); otherwise `precision(C): not-available (incomplete negative universe)` with two labelled figures beside it, `precisionAmongJudgedTracks(C)` (the same ratio over judgeable tracks) and the count of C-predictions on `vehicle-unresolved` tracks. `recall(C)` = assigned exact-C pairs whose MAVI outcome is C ÷ assigned exact-C pairs (an `undetermined` outcome counts in the denominator, so abstention lowers recall; it is Track-conditional subclass recall, scored over assigned pairs only as §8 scope B states); `support(C)` = assigned exact-C pairs; unmatched, ambiguous, fragmented, merged and unverified exact-C GT are detection or tracking outcomes that stay in scope A and never enter recall or support, so a missed vehicle cannot be reported as a misclassified one; recall never includes subset or unsupported tracks; macro averages run only over classes present with an `exact` mapping and adequate support; a mapping file naming a native class the adapter never emits, or omitting one it does emit, is refused (`mapping_incomplete`); kinds are declared, never inferred. The dataset-level coverage summary (`full`/`partial`/`none`, as in the capability matrix) is derived for reporting and is not an input.

**Worked example** (dataset classes `car`, `bus`, `van`, `other`; MAVI `car | truck | bus | motorcycle`): `car → car exact`, `bus → bus exact`, `van → unsupported` (body not resolved), `other → unsupported`. `van` and `other` are `vehicle-unresolved`. Scope B reports car and bus **recall**; `precision(car)` is available only if no van or other track was predicted `car`, else `not-available` with `precisionAmongJudgedTracks(car)` and the count of `car` predictions on van/other tracks; the same for bus; truck and motorcycle have `recall: not-in-dataset`, while their precision is judgeable like any class (a MAVI `truck` on an exact `car` track is a false positive for `precision(truck)`; a class never predicted gets `no-judgeable-predictions`); van and other tracks appear in coverage and in the excluded counts; a MAVI `truck` on a `van` is unjudgeable and counted as such. By contrast, in a dataset whose only unsupported classes are `outside-capability` (BDD100K: pedestrian, rider, bicycle, train), every assigned track is judgeable, so precision is available for every class, and a MAVI Vehicle Track assigned to a pedestrian that predicts `car` is a counted false positive for `car`. **Second example** (dataset classes `sedan`, `van`, `other`): `sedan → car subset`, `van → unsupported`, `other → unsupported`. Scope B reports the sedan subset-conditional block; `recall(car)` is `not-in-dataset` (no exact `car` class); `precision(car)` is `not-available` whenever a van or other track was predicted `car`, and when none was, the available `precision(car)` is explicitly a figure over sedans only (the report names the judgeable classes behind every precision), so this dataset supports no full-class `car` recall claim and no unqualified `car` precision claim.

## 7. GT ↔ MAVI Track association (`capabilities/vehicle_tracks/association.py`)

**Inputs** (geometry and time only): per sequence, GT tracks as `{gtTrackId, frames[] {frameIndex, videoOffsetMs, box (normalised x, y, w, h), ignore: bool}}` (`videoOffsetMs` is the labelled instant as a media-relative offset into the derived video MAVI processed, the same clock as the trajectory `offsetMs`; it is never a wall-clock or absolute instant, per the AGENTS time-naming rules) with class **stripped before** the association call (the ground-truth loader passes a projection that has no class field; a test asserts the projection's field set). **Frame-level `ignore`:** a frame flagged `ignore: true` is removed from that GT track before any computation — it is not one of the track's labelled frames for alignment, `overlapFrames`, `gtCoverage`, continuity or the IoU gate — and its box is added to that frame's ignore boxes, so a MAVI centre there is neither contained by nor counted against the track and contributes to `ignoredMavi`. `gtCoverage` is therefore over the track's non-ignored frames. A GT track left with fewer than `minOverlapFrames` non-ignored frames is an **ignored track**: listed in `ignoredGt[]`, in no GT population and no denominator of scope A, never assigned. Partially ignored tracks are otherwise ordinary GT. Only this rule decides whether a track is ignored; adapters set the per-frame flag and never drop tracks themselves; MAVI Tracks as `{maviTrackId, points[] {offsetMs, cx, cy}, observations[] {offsetMs, box}}` with subclass and confidence **absent** from the projection. The export carries Person and Vehicle Tracks; the MAVI loader applies the capability selection **before** projecting, keeping only Tracks whose `objectClass` is `Vehicle`, so Person Tracks never enter association, any count or any denominator (a Person Track on a pedestrian is correct Stage-2 behaviour and is not this capability's evidence; a Vehicle Track on a pedestrian is, and must not be able to hide behind a Person Track that would otherwise take the pairing). `objectClass` is the broad class of the capability, not a subclass, and the selected set is logged by count; the geometry projection after selection has no class field. Ignore regions are passed as boxes, not tracks, and only from label semantics the capability matrix records as **verified** against the dataset's own label specification (VisDrone category 0 and score 0 are verified ignore semantics; KITTI `DontCare` likewise). BDD100K `crowd` is **not yet verified** (the matrix lists its semantics as unresolved): the BDD100K adapter emits no ignore box from `crowd` and treats such objects as ordinary GT of their class until the matrix row moves `crowd` to verified facts with its source; a dataset descriptor that declares an ignore mapping the matrix has not verified is refused (`ignore_semantics_unverified`). Suppressing GT or Vehicle Tracks on an attribute whose meaning is a guess would distort coverage and precision silently, so the harness refuses rather than assumes. All GT boxes of a frame are visible to the per-frame step together, so containment can be made exclusive (§7.2).

**7.1 Time alignment and evaluable points.** The adapter declares the labelled instants (`frameTime`) and the derivation manifest declares the source rate of the video MAVI processed. Two source modes exist: **labelled-rate video** (the MP4 is encoded from exactly the labelled frames, so every MAVI frame is a labelled instant; 5 Hz for BDD100K MOT and VisDrone sequences) and **higher-rate source video** (an identity-aligned original clip, for example 30 fps with 5 Hz labels). In both, the labelled instants are the sequence's actual label times: `index-at-fps` gives `t_k = k × 1000 × fpsDen / fpsNum` (kept as an exact rational, not rounded, until comparison), `per-frame-timestamp` gives the recorded times. Each labelled instant owns a **half-open alignment window whose boundaries are the midpoints between consecutive actual labelled instants**: instant `t_k` owns `[ (t_(k−1) + t_k)/2 , (t_k + t_(k+1))/2 )`, the first window starts at `t_0 − (t_1 − t_0)/2` and the last ends at `t_n + (t_n − t_(n−1))/2`, and a point exactly on a boundary belongs to the later instant (half-open). The windows therefore tile the labelled span contiguously with no gaps and no overlaps for any interval, integral or not (33⅓ ms at 30 fps included) and for irregular per-frame timestamps alike; a trajectory point belongs to exactly one window. A sequence with a single labelled instant uses `±Δ/2` from the declared rate. Membership in a window is the complete alignment rule; the policy carries no separate time tolerance, so two implementations cannot disagree on which points are evaluable. For each labelled instant, the **evaluable MAVI point** of a Track is the point in its window nearest to the instant, if any; on an exact tie the earlier offset wins. A Track's `evaluablePoints` are exactly those points, one per labelled instant it covers, and no point can support two instants (so two points can never produce three aligned frames). MAVI points at unlabelled times are never evaluable and never count for or against a Track. Frame-rate differences are handled by time, never by frame-index arithmetic across rates. The higher-rate mode is used only when the release provides a defensible identity mapping between the source clip and the labelled frames; correspondence is never assumed from a shared file name alone.

**7.2 Per-frame evidence.** For each labelled GT frame that has an evaluable MAVI point (§7.1; there is no other time predicate): `d` = Euclidean distance between the MAVI centre and the GT box centre, divided by the GT box diagonal (both normalised); `inside` = the MAVI centre lies within the GT box **and** this GT box is the one with the smallest `d` among all GT boxes of that frame that contain the centre (nearest-centre exclusivity). In a queue or platoon where boxes overlap, a centre therefore supports at most one GT per frame, and a centre sitting in the overlap of two boxes supports the nearer one. At each MAVI observation frame (≤ 4 per Track), `iou` = IoU between the observation box and the GT box **at the observation time**: the labelled box when the observation falls on a labelled instant (always, in labelled-rate mode), otherwise the box linearly interpolated between the two bracketing labelled frames of the same GT track (higher-rate mode; interpolation across a 200 ms gap is a deterministic, geometric estimate and is recorded as such). An observation outside the GT track's labelled span has no `iou` for that GT; nor has an observation whose alignment window (§7.1) belongs to a frame the GT track flags `ignore`, or one that would have to be interpolated across such a frame (the ignored frame is removed before box lookup, and interpolation never spans a removed frame) — such observations are excluded from the IoU gate rather than judged against ignored evidence. Box conventions are converted by the adapter (pixel `x1 y1 x2 y2` or `left top width height` to normalised `x y w h` using the declared frame size); the adapter is tested on that conversion.

**7.3 Pair score.** For a candidate pair: `overlapFrames` = GT frames with an aligned MAVI point; `containment` = inside ÷ overlapFrames; `meanNormalisedDistance`; `gtCoverage` = contained frames ÷ **all non-ignored** GT frames of the track (how much of the object's labelled lifetime this MAVI Track spatially covers; ignored frames are absent, §7 inputs); `maviCoverage` = contained evaluable points ÷ **the MAVI Track's evaluable points, excluding those aligned to this GT track's ignored frames** (§7.1; a point on an instant the GT flags `ignore` counts against nothing, so it leaves the denominator for this pair exactly as the frame leaves the GT side), i.e. the share of the Track's GT-observable life that lies on this object: its purity. Unlabelled MAVI points do not enter either term, so a geometrically perfect 30 fps Track against 5 Hz labels has `maviCoverage` 1.0, and a 30 fps Track that follows another object at most labelled instants fails the purity floor. `spotCheckIoU[]` = the `iou` values at the pair's observation frames (§7.2). Both coverages are spatial (contained points), never merely temporal. Candidates are generated only for pairs whose time spans overlap (interval sort, §7.5).

**7.4 Assignment and outcomes** (policy v1 defaults; chosen from geometry and synthetic fixtures, §7.6):
- Eligible pair, all of: `overlapFrames ≥ minOverlapFrames (3)`; `containment ≥ minContainment (0.5)`; `gtCoverage ≥ minGtCoverage (0.5)` (a short MAVI fragment on a long GT is not an assignment); `maviCoverage ≥ minMaviCoverage (0.5)` (a MAVI Track that spends most of its life on another object is not an assignment); the **continuity rule**: over the GT track's **non-ignored labelled frames in order** (not merely the frames that happen to have a MAVI point), there must be a run of at least `minConsecutiveContainedFrames (3)` **adjacent labelled frames** each of which has an evaluable MAVI point that is contained; a labelled frame with no evaluable point breaks the run exactly as an aligned-but-not-contained frame does (a trajectory inside the GT box on frames 1, 3, 5, … and elsewhere on 2, 4, 6, … has containment, `gtCoverage` and `maviCoverage` all 0.5 but a longest run of one frame, and is not an assignment; a trajectory with points only on frames 1, 3, 5 of six, all contained, has `overlapFrames` 3 and containment 1.0 but a longest run of one frame, and is not an assignment either; a Track contained on frames 1–4, out on 5, contained on 6–10 has a run of five and is eligible, the dropout costing it only containment; a Track contained for a continuous first half and then gone has a long run and is judged by the merged/fragment rules of §7.4; ignored frames (§7 inputs) are absent from the sequence and neither break nor extend a run); and the **IoU gate**: at least one aligned observation frame exists and every aligned observation has `iou ≥ minSpotCheckIoU (0.3)`. A pair that satisfies **every other** eligibility condition above (`minOverlapFrames`, containment, both coverage floors and the continuity rule) but fails the IoU gate (including having no observation with an `iou` for that GT) is a **verification-failed pair**: never assigned, recorded on both sides (§7.4 outcomes). A pair that fails any non-IoU condition is simply ineligible, whatever its IoU, and never makes either side `unverified`.
- Order eligible pairs by (`containment` desc, `overlapFrames` desc, `meanNormalisedDistance` asc, `gtTrackId`, `maviTrackId`): fully deterministic.
- Greedy one-to-one: take pairs in order, skipping any whose GT or MAVI Track is already assigned.
- Ambiguity: before accepting the best pair (*g*, *m*), another unassigned eligible MAVI Track *m′* competes for *g* only if it is comparable on **every** dimension the ordering uses: `containment ≥ best − ambiguityMargin (0.1)` **and** `gtCoverage ≥ best.gtCoverage − ambiguityMargin` (overlap over the same GT, so this is the overlap dimension) **and** `meanNormalisedDistance ≤ best.meanNormalisedDistance + distanceAmbiguityMargin (0.1, in GT-diagonal units)`; then *g* is `ambiguous` and neither candidate is assigned to it (both stay available for other GT). A candidate equal in containment and purity but covering clearly less of *g* (a duplicate fragment beside a full-lifetime Track) is not a competitor: the best pair is assigned and the fragment becomes `fragmentMavi`. A candidate with equal containment and coverage that sits clearly farther from the GT centre (near the box edge while the best stays near the centre) is likewise not a competitor. Symmetric rule for a MAVI Track with two near-equal GT candidates (crossing objects), comparable on containment, `maviCoverage` and distance.
- After assignment, every remaining GT and MAVI Track is classified from **spatial** pair evidence only (the same per-frame evidence as above; temporal co-occurrence alone never classifies anything). GT states are **mutually exclusive by precedence**: an unassigned GT takes the first state whose condition holds in the order `ambiguousGt` → `mergedGt` → `fragmentedGt` → `unverifiedGt` → `unmatchedGt` (an identity merge is reported ahead of a split, which is reported ahead of a verification failure), so every GT appears in exactly one list and bucket sums are set cardinalities. MAVI states use the order `fragmentMavi` → `ignoredMavi` → `unverifiedMavi` → `unmatchedMavi`, likewise exclusive:
  - `fragmentMavi`: an unassigned MAVI Track with `containment ≥ 0.5`, `overlapFrames ≥ 3` and `maviCoverage ≥ 0.5` against an **assigned** GT (MAVI split the object; coverage scope only).
  - `fragmentedGt`: an unassigned GT with no eligible single pair that has **at least two distinct** contained MAVI fragments (each with `containment ≥ 0.5`, `overlapFrames ≥ 3`) which together cover `≥ minGtCoverage` of its frames: MAVI saw the object but as several Tracks; not assigned, so it never enters scope B. A single Track that fails eligibility (for example on the continuity rule) is not a split and never makes its GT `fragmentedGt`; that GT falls through to `unverifiedGt` or `unmatchedGt`.
  - `mergedGt`: an unassigned GT that has a pair with an **assigned** MAVI Track meeting `containment ≥ 0.5`, `overlapFrames ≥ 3` and `gtCoverage ≥ 0.5` (the MAVI Track passed through this object too, but was assigned elsewhere): MAVI merged identities. A GT that merely overlaps in time with an assigned Track is **not** merged.
  - `ignoredMavi`: an unassigned MAVI Track with ≥ 50 % of its **evaluable** points (§7.1, one per labelled instant; raw centres at unlabelled times never count, so the rate is the same at 5 Hz and 30 fps) inside the ignore boxes of their instants.
  - `unverifiedGt` (GT side): an unassigned GT that is not ambiguous, fragmented or merged and has at least one verification-failed pair; one GT counts once however many MAVI Tracks failed verification against it.
  - `unverifiedMavi` (MAVI side): an unassigned MAVI Track with at least one verification-failed pair; a MAVI-side diagnostic population, never added to GT-side counts.
  - `unmatchedGt` / `unmatchedMavi`: everything else.
- The spot-check IoUs are part of eligibility (the IoU gate above) and are also reported per pair, so a reviewer can see the box evidence behind every assignment.
- **Class leakage protection:** the association module imports nothing from the subclass evaluator, its input projections have no class or confidence field (asserted by test), and the mutation test in §9 proves that changing every MAVI subclass and every native class leaves the class-free association body (`sequences[]`, `associationBodySha256`) byte-identical, while the envelope's `groundTruthSha256`, `mappingSha256` and `benchmarkRunId` legitimately change.

**7.5 Method choice.** Compared: (a) greedy best-score matching; (b) Hungarian global assignment on a containment matrix; (c) frame-level matching accumulated to track level; (d) track-level overlap matrix then assignment. (c) is what §7.2–7.3 compute, so the real choice is between (a) and (b) on the pair-score matrix. Hungarian maximises the *sum* of scores and can accept a weak pair to free a strong one, which is hard to explain in an evidence report and interacts badly with explicit ambiguity rejection; greedy with the ambiguity rule accepts only pairs that are individually strong and unambiguous, which is what a reviewer must be able to verify by hand from the artefact. **Choice: greedy with explicit ambiguity rejection** (a). Complexity: candidate generation by sorting both track sets by start time and sweeping, O((G + M) log(G + M) + P) for P temporally overlapping pairs; scoring O(P × F) for F aligned frames per pair; assignment O(P log P). Hungarian (O(n³)) is kept as a documented alternative if ambiguity rates in real data prove greedy inadequate; switching would be a new policy version, recorded in the envelope.

**7.6 Threshold provenance.** `minContainment` 0.5, `minGtCoverage` 0.5 and `minMaviCoverage` 0.5 (an object must have the Track on it for at least half of its labelled lifetime, and the Track must be on the object for at least half of its GT-observable lifetime), `minOverlapFrames` 3, `minSpotCheckIoU` 0.3 (a detector box and a GT box of the same object overlap well above this even under loose boxes; two adjacent vehicles rarely reach it), `ambiguityMargin` 0.1 are set from geometric reasoning and validated on the synthetic fixtures of §9 (crossing, queue, fragments, merges, jitter). They are never chosen from benchmark subclass outcomes. Any change is a new policy version with its own fixtures.

**7.7 Limitation and escalation.** With centre-only trajectories, per-frame IoU is available only at the ≤ 4 observation frames; the rest of the overlap is checked by exclusive containment and the two coverage floors. This satisfies ADR-017 §6's evidence list (time overlap, IoU where boxes exist, continuity, one-to-one constraints, support and purity) at the strength the stored data allows, and the association artefact says so. **Escalation trigger, declared now (one population):** the **coverage-limited GT rate** = (`ambiguousGt` + `fragmentedGt` + `mergedGt` + `unverifiedGt`) ÷ expected-vehicle GT tracks (§8 scope A), numerator restricted to the same population; every term is a count of GT tracks and the states are mutually exclusive by the precedence in §7.4, so the sum is the size of the union and each GT counts once. If on the first real benchmark it exceeds 10 %, the result is reported as coverage-limited and the owner decides, before H3 relies on it, whether to add per-point boxes to the trajectory artefact (a vision-worker and evidence-format change with its own ADR note, Track-identity-gate re-pin and tests) or to accept the limitation. The trigger is a reporting rule; it does not alter the association. The MAVI-side rate `unverifiedMavi` ÷ all MAVI Tracks is reported beside it as a diagnostic and never enters the trigger.

**Limitation stated in every association artefact:** see §7.7. Nearest-centre exclusivity, the coverage floors and the IoU gate remove the easy failure modes (adjacent boxes, short fragments, merged Tracks, a centre parked in the wrong box); what remains is reported, counted and, past the trigger, escalated.

## 8. Evaluation semantics (`capabilities/vehicle_subclass/evaluate.py`)

Two scopes, two sections, two scope statements (S3.2 plan §2 and §14).

**Scope A — end-to-end association coverage (detection and tracking; not subclass accuracy).** Association (§7) runs class-free over every GT track that is not an ignored track (frame-level ignore rule, §7 inputs; `ignoredGt` is reported as a count and is in no population below); scope A then partitions GT by mapping for reporting. **Expected-vehicle GT** = non-ignored GT whose native class maps `exact`, `subset` or `unsupported (vehicle-unresolved)`; **outside-capability GT** = non-ignored GT mapped `unsupported (outside-capability)`, retained as negatives, never in vehicle denominators. Per sequence and overall: expected-vehicle GT total, assigned, ambiguous, fragmented, merged, unverified, unmatched; association rate = assigned expected-vehicle GT ÷ expected-vehicle GT; the coverage-limited GT rate (§7.7, over expected-vehicle GT); outside-capability GT total and `vehicleTracksOnOutsideCapabilityGt` (MAVI Vehicle Tracks assigned to them: detection or tracking errors made visible, not coverage); MAVI Tracks total, assigned, fragment, unverified, ignored, unmatched, and the MAVI-side unverified rate; the alignment block (source rate, labelled rate, evaluable and total MAVI points); per native class (including `unsupported` ones) the same GT counts, so a class MAVI never tracks is visible here.

**Scope B — Track-conditional subclass quality (on assigned pairs only).** Each assigned pair yields (native class, mapping, MAVI outcome) where the MAVI outcome is `_track_outcome`: a MAVI class or `undetermined`. Then: confusion over MAVI classes for `exact` GT only; a **subset-conditional block** per `subset` native class (assigned tracks, share resolved to the mapped class, `undetermined` share, confusion of MAVI outcomes on that subset), kept outside the exact-GT confusion and every recall figure while its judgeable truths enter class precision (§6); `excluded` counts for `unsupported` GT; per class recall, support (assigned exact-GT pair count, §6), `undetermined` share (abstention coverage), accuracy over resolved and over evaluable, all from assigned `exact` GT pairs only; per class precision over all judgeable predictions (exact, subset and outside-capability truths), available per class only when no `vehicle-unresolved` track received that prediction (§6), otherwise `not-available` with the labelled conditional figure and the unjudgeable-prediction count, and always naming the judgeable native classes behind it; `recall: not-in-dataset` for classes without exact GT (precision is independent of exact GT: `not-available` first whenever any prediction of the class is unjudgeable, `no-judgeable-predictions` when the class was predicted on no assigned track at all, else computed, in that order per §6); `insufficient-support` below the registered floor of 30 (`run_subclass_measurement.status` ordering; the floor is read from the registered requirements and recorded by hash, so benchmark and pilot speak the same language; no operational pass or fail is claimed, as every operational minimum is `null`). Macro figures are computed only over classes with exact GT and adequate support, and the result lists which classes they cover.

**Partial-taxonomy guarantees:** no recall for a class without exact GT; no class precision while any prediction of that class landed on an unjudgeable (`vehicle-unresolved`) track (only the labelled conditional figure); known negatives (outside-capability and other-class judgeable tracks) always count in precision denominators; no coercion of `unsupported` classes; `subset` evidence stays in its own block for recall and confusion, enters class precision only as judgeable truth, and never enters class recall; excluded counts always visible; dataset coverage summary derived and labelled.

## 11. CLI flow (`python -m tools.benchmarks.cli …`; Windows-first)

1. `describe --descriptor <release.json>`: validate the descriptor and mapping, print status and preconditions; refuse `BLOCKED`. `describe --freeze-manifest --source-root <source>` computes the source manifest (every file: path, size, SHA-256) and writes the frozen descriptor (write-once); this step is mandatory after acquisition.
2. `prepare --descriptor <frozen> --source-root … --split val --out <derived>`: refuses a descriptor whose manifest is empty (`source_manifest_missing`); reconciles the **entire** frozen manifest against the source root before anything else: every manifest entry must exist with the recorded size and SHA-256 (`source_manifest_incomplete` for a missing member, `source_manifest_mismatch` for a differing one), and any file under the declared split that the manifest does not list is `source_manifest_unexpected`; only then does the adapter discover sequences, and the discovered set must be exactly the sequences the manifest's members describe (`source_manifest_incomplete` otherwise), so a deleted annotation file and its frames cannot silently shrink the split while the descriptor still claims the complete frozen manifest; every file read afterwards is re-verified on read; encodes derived MP4s with the verified FFmpeg pack; writes ground truth per sequence (canonical) and a derivation manifest naming the source hashes; refuses an existing output. The result envelope then binds the frozen descriptor hash, which covers the source manifest, and the hashes of the canonical prepared ground truth and the derivation manifest, so a result is bound to exact source bytes and to the exact truth it was scored against, not to a claimed release; two runs whose prepared ground truth differs in a single native class cannot share a `benchmarkRunId`.
3. `execute --derived … --api http://localhost:<port> --journal … --export-exe …`: the T9 pattern (shared `Api`): fresh-catalogue check, one camera per sequence, import, process, journal before polling, T1 export per run, then locate and copy each `trajectorySha256` from the evidence root (`--evidence-root`), verifying bytes. Resumable from the journal only within the same catalogue; refuses pre-existing assets.
4. `evaluate --derived … --mavi … --mapping … --policy … --out <results/run>`: association then evaluation, both scopes, report; everything write-once; `benchmarkRunId` printed.
Refusals use the `S32Error`-style `refused <code>` convention. `prepare` and `evaluate` are deterministic and regenerable into a new output directory; `execute` is journalled like T9. The subclass predictions are first read by `evaluate`'s scope-B step, after association has been written; `associate` is not a separate command, but the association artefact is written and hashed before scope B runs, so the mutation test can prove independence on the artefact.

Fallback if BDD100K cannot be acquired: VisDrone MOT (aerial; `car, van, truck, bus, motor` evaluated; `motor → motorcycle exact` to verify) as primary with `van` and aerial caveats, supplemented by UAVDT (aerial; `car, truck, bus`) — a combination (ADR-017 §3) rather than waiting for one dataset.

**Domain diversity (H4 candidates, later):** fixed-camera traffic: UA-DETRAC (`car, bus` exact if a credible copy with establishable identity exists; original site offline); aerial: UAVDT (`car, truck, bus`; "research purpose only"; Google Drive) and VisDrone MOT. KITTI tracking (registration; `Car, Truck` exact, `Van` unsupported) is a weaker third. nuScenes is noted for its explicit definitions (car includes vans and SUVs; truck includes pickups; motorcycle includes scooters) but its annotations are 3D cuboids needing projection, so it is not a first-choice 2D tracking benchmark. MIO-TCD and similar still-image sets could support component-level classification checks, not Track evidence.

## 13. Exposure and contamination metadata

The envelope's `exposure` block: `{status: known | possible | unknown | none-known, basis, notes}` per dataset, populated from the model card and MAVI component history before a result is reported; interpretation text in the report: "Development/reference evidence; not independent generalisation evidence" when status is not `none-known`. A possible exposure never discards a benchmark; it changes the sentence under the table.

## 14. Deterministic failure modes

`descriptor_invalid`, `descriptor_blocked`, `source_manifest_missing`, `source_manifest_mismatch`, `source_manifest_incomplete`, `source_manifest_unexpected`, `mapping_invalid`, `mapping_incomplete`, `adapter_label_invalid`, `adapter_frame_missing`, `ignore_semantics_unverified` (descriptor declares an ignore mapping the capability matrix has not verified), `derivation_pack_mismatch`, `export_invalid` (from `artefacts`), `trajectory_missing`, `trajectory_hash_mismatch`, `trajectory_format_unsupported` (not v1), `producer_mixed` (exports attest different producers), `profile_mismatch`, `association_policy_invalid`, `tooling_dirty` (a behaviour-bearing harness file differs from `toolingCommit`), `output_exists`. Each refusal names the sequence or file; nothing is written on refusal.

## 15. Performance

BDD100K MOT val is on the order of two hundred videos of about two hundred frames at 5 Hz; GT tracks per video are tens to low hundreds, MAVI Tracks similar. Candidate pairs after temporal pruning are at most a few thousand per video, each scored over ≤ 200 frames: pure-Python association runs in seconds per video and minutes for the split. Goal: `evaluate` for the val split completes in under 30 minutes on the Development machine without optimisation; `prepare` and `execute` are dominated by encoding and MAVI processing (hours, like T9).

## 18. Risks and open questions

- **Centre-only trajectories** limit association strength (§7.7). Mitigation: exclusive containment, coverage floors on both sides, the IoU gate at observation frames, honest counts, and the declared escalation trigger to trajectory v2.
- **Frame rate.** If only 5 Hz labelled frames can be run, tracker behaviour is measured in a 5 Hz domain and said so; if 30 fps clips are used, their identity mapping to the labelled frames must be verifiable from the release (§12).
- **BDD100K definitions** for `truck` and `motorcycle` are unverified; the mapping kind may become `subset`.
- **Acquisition** needs a manual licence acceptance (owner or operator); nothing in the harness bypasses it.
- **UA-DETRAC availability** is uncertain; H4 does not depend on it.
- **Owner decisions required now:** none for implementing slices 1–3. Before slice 5: accept the BDD100K licence and confirm the val split as the first primary benchmark, or choose the VisDrone + UAVDT combination.
